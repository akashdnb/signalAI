## Find a conversation

1. Open Inbox.
2. Choose All, Unread, AI Handling, Qualified, or Needs Attention.
3. Search by Instagram username if needed.
4. Select a conversation to view its timeline.

While Inbox is open, the conversation list refreshes periodically. If you expect a recent event and do not see it yet, wait for the next refresh and check the connected account before assuming it was lost.

## Understand the handoff state

- **AI is replying:** the conversation is currently assigned to AI handling.
- **Escalated — needs attention:** attention has been requested and a teammate should review the conversation.
- **Human is replying:** a teammate has taken over the conversation.

The Unread, AI Handling, Qualified, and Needs Attention filters are queues, not separate copies of a conversation.

## Take over and reply

1. Open the conversation.
2. Choose Take over to reply.
3. Wait for the handoff state to update.
4. Compose and send the human reply.
5. Review the timeline to confirm the reply appears.

Taking over pauses AI handling so a teammate can continue the conversation. If sending fails, read the error, retain the draft text, and check the conversation state before retrying.

## Return control to AI or request attention

The lead detail page provides controls to return a human-handled conversation to AI or escalate an AI-handled conversation for attention. Use the control deliberately and verify the resulting handoff label.

Before returning control, make sure the AI's trigger, goals, and knowledge still make sense for that conversation.

## Coordinate team follow-up

Assign an owner and update the pipeline stage on the lead detail page. Use internal notes and tags to record context, next steps, and why the conversation was escalated. Avoid asking a prospect to repeat details already visible in the thread.

## Best practices

- Take over promptly when a person asks for a human, the AI is uncertain, or a conversation needs judgment.
- Read the recent thread and captured facts before replying so the prospect does not have to repeat themselves.
- Assign an owner for escalated or high-intent conversations and leave a clear next action.
- Return control to AI only when the journey can safely continue; verify the state after changing it.
- Avoid sending duplicate replies during retries; check the timeline and handoff status first.

## If messages are missing

Check the current handoff state, account connection, and conversation timeline. Troubleshooting explains how to distinguish missing event delivery from a journey that did not match or a reply that failed.

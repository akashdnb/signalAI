## What SignalAI does

SignalAI helps a team respond to relevant Instagram comments and direct messages, continue conversations with a rule-based reply or an AI-generated reply, and organize useful prospect information as leads.

A journey defines what starts a conversation, the first response, and the qualification goals the conversation should work toward. Review resulting conversations in Inbox and prospects in Leads.

## The core flow

Instagram comment or DM → keyword trigger → reply → qualification goals → captured lead information → human follow-up when needed.

## Key terms

- **Journey:** a configured automation with a trigger, reply behavior, and optional qualification goals.
- **Trigger:** the Instagram activity that can start a journey, such as a comment or direct message matching a keyword.
- **Goal or milestone:** a qualification checkpoint with a description and optional fields to capture.
- **Captured field:** a structured value such as phone, location, budget, or buying intent.
- **Handoff:** a change from AI handling to a teammate handling the conversation.

## Best practices

- Start with one measurable business outcome, such as sending a brochure or qualifying a property enquiry.
- Keep the first journey small. Add complexity only after real conversations show that it is needed.
- Check the conversation and captured facts, not only the number of replies, when judging quality.
- Use human follow-up for high-value or ambiguous cases rather than trying to automate every edge case.

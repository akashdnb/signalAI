## Open and filter leads

1. Open Leads from the dashboard.
2. Search by Instagram username.
3. Filter by pipeline stage or handoff status.
4. Select a lead to open its detail page.

The list shows username, pipeline stage, handoff state, latest contact time and channel, messaging-window state, and first-seen time.

## Use the pipeline

- **New:** the prospect has been recorded but has not yet advanced.
- **Contacted:** a teammate has started follow-up.
- **Qualified:** the prospect meets your team's qualification threshold.
- **Meeting Scheduled:** a meeting or visit is arranged.
- **Won:** the opportunity converted.
- **Lost:** the opportunity is no longer active.

These stages are team workflow labels, not proof that SignalAI independently verified a business outcome. Update them based on what your team learns.

## Work from the lead detail page

- **Overview:** review status, owner, tags, captured facts, and available lead-intelligence details.
- **Conversation:** inspect the message and event timeline.
- **Activities:** review timeline activity relevant to the lead.
- **Notes:** keep internal context for the team.

Where controls are available, you can assign an owner, change the pipeline stage, add or remove tags, add notes, and create or update a deal.

## Use captured facts responsibly

Review captured fields alongside the conversation that produced them. If a value is unclear, correct journey configuration and ask a human to verify the data. Collect only information needed to qualify or follow up with the prospect.

## Messaging window

The Leads table shows whether the stored messaging-window indicator is open or closed. Treat that indicator as operational context, not a guarantee that every type of outbound message is permitted. Follow Instagram and Meta messaging rules when replying.

## Best practices

- Agree on what each pipeline stage means so teammates update leads consistently.
- Assign an owner when a person needs follow-up and record the next step in a note.
- Verify phone numbers, budgets, and intent against the conversation before acting on them.
- Use tags consistently; define a small team vocabulary rather than inventing near-duplicate tags.
- Keep internal notes focused on useful business context and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information.
- Track qualified leads and follow-up outcomes, not just the number of conversations.
